\begin{abstract}
金融大规模异常检测不仅要从极低异常比例的数据中识别高风险对象，还要满足近实时场景对吞吐和时延的要求。随着样本量由百万级向千万级、亿级扩展，系统瓶颈逐渐集中到四个环节：全量异常评分开销高，候选召回在外存访问条件下面临吞吐约束，中央处理器（CPU）与图形处理器（GPU）之间的数据交换会放大阶段边界成本，多GPU并行还受到结果归并、共享输入输出（I/O）路径和硬件拓扑的影响。基于这一判断，本文将研究重点放在大规模金融异常检测的系统组织与执行效率上，目标是在尽量保持检测效果的同时降低端到端开销，并提高单机多GPU条件下的扩展能力。

本文设计并实现了两阶段异常检测系统 FlashTOD。第一阶段采用外存近似最近邻搜索（ANNS）系统 FlashANNS，从全量样本中召回候选；第二阶段采用张量异常评分框架 PyTOD 对候选样本评分，输出异常分数及风险排名前 $k$ 位的对象。候选预算决定进入精细评分阶段的样本范围。系统按查询保留候选编号、距离和局部排序与原始样本的对应关系，在设备侧构造评分张量，使检索结果能直接用于后续评分。

单GPU场景下，本文围绕候选召回到异常评分之间的热路径进行数据路径优化。针对查询准备、候选重组和评分输入过程中频繁的CPU–GPU往返与阶段同步，构建GPU主导的执行链路，使查询向量、候选编号、局部距离和评分中间张量尽量在设备侧连续传递，并通过异步I/O与计算重叠、缓冲区复用等方式减少等待。多GPU场景则采用数据并行与索引复制的架构，使每张GPU独立完成本地候选召回和异常评分，CPU主要负责请求调度和小规模结果汇总；对于需要跨卡协同的场景，进一步结合设备侧归并、通信和拓扑感知I/O控制，缓解共享路径竞争对吞吐和尾延迟的影响。

实验采用约百万（1M）规模的真实或半真实金融数据验证有效性，并使用10M、50M和100M高保真合成数据测试吞吐、延迟与扩展趋势。在 1M 合成桥接实验中，完整融合方案的精确率—召回率曲线下面积（PR-AUC）为0.906，接近PyTOD全量评分的0.932；每秒查询数（QPS）由2700提升至4280，增幅约58.5\%。单卡完整优化方案将QPS从3180提高到4550，同时把第99百分位延迟（p99）由67.0 ms降至45.5 ms。10M 索引复制路径下，1、2、4 GPU的QPS分别为4550、7780和13620，4 GPU吞吐约为单GPU的2.99倍。

实验结果说明，FlashTOD在保持较好检测效果的同时，能够有效压缩单GPU执行链路中的数据传输与同步开销，并在多GPU条件下获得可验证的吞吐扩展收益。相关结论限定在本文给定的数据规模、候选预算和硬件平台范围内；更复杂的真实生产负载以及多机分布式场景，仍需进一步验证。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
Large-scale outlier detection for financial risk control must identify high-risk objects from extremely imbalanced data with a vanishingly small outlier ratio, while meeting near-real-time throughput and tail-latency requirements. As the sample size scales from millions to tens or hundreds of millions, system bottlenecks increasingly concentrate in four parts: full-scan anomaly scoring is costly, candidate retrieval becomes throughput-limited under out-of-core access, data exchange between the central processing unit (CPU) and graphics processing unit (GPU) amplifies stage-boundary overhead, and multi-GPU parallelism is constrained by result aggregation, shared input/output (I/O) paths, and hardware topology. Based on these observations, this thesis focuses on system organization and execution efficiency for large-scale financial outlier detection, aiming to reduce end-to-end cost while largely preserving detection effectiveness and improving scalability on a single multi-GPU machine.

To address these issues, this thesis designs and implements \emph{FlashTOD}, a collaborative outlier detection system that couples the PyTOD tensor-based scoring framework with FlashANNS, an out-of-core approximate nearest neighbor search (ANNS) system. FlashTOD separates candidate retrieval from anomaly scoring: FlashANNS first generates a size-controlled candidate set from large-scale samples, and PyTOD then performs tensorized anomaly scoring within the candidate set and outputs anomaly scores and Top-$k$ risky objects. By limiting the scoring scope via a candidate budget, FlashTOD transforms expensive full-scan scoring into refined scoring over candidates, enabling a tunable trade-off between effectiveness and system cost. A key contribution is the interface integration and data organization that connect candidate retrieval and anomaly scoring along a unified execution chain.

On a single GPU, we optimize the hot path from candidate retrieval to anomaly scoring. To mitigate frequent CPU--GPU round trips and stage synchronization during query preparation, candidate regrouping, and scoring-input construction, we build a GPU-led execution chain so that query vectors, candidate IDs, local distances, and intermediate scoring tensors are transferred continuously on device whenever possible. We further reduce waiting by overlapping asynchronous I/O with computation and reusing buffers. On multiple GPUs, FlashTOD adopts a replicated (data-parallel, index-replicated) architecture: each GPU independently completes local candidate retrieval and anomaly scoring, while the CPU mainly handles request scheduling and lightweight result aggregation. For scenarios requiring cross-device collaboration, we additionally incorporate device-side aggregation, communication, and topology-aware I/O control to alleviate throughput and tail-latency degradation due to shared-path contention.

Experiments use real or semi-real financial datasets at around 1M scale to validate effectiveness, and high-fidelity synthetic datasets at 10M, 50M, and 100M scales to evaluate throughput, latency, and scalability trends. In the 1M synthetic bridging experiment, the full system achieves an area under the precision-recall curve (PR-AUC) of 0.906, close to the 0.932 of the PyTOD full-scoring baseline. Throughput in queries per second (QPS) increases from 2700 to 4280 (\,+58.5\%). The full single-GPU optimization increases QPS from 3180 to 4550 and reduces the 99th-percentile (p99) latency from 67.0 ms to 45.5 ms. In the 10M index-replication experiment, QPS on 1, 2, and 4 GPUs reaches 4550, 7780, and 13620, respectively, with 4 GPUs achieving about 2.99$\times$ the single-GPU throughput.

These results show that FlashTOD maintains strong detection effectiveness while effectively reducing data-movement and synchronization overhead on a single GPU, and achieves verifiable throughput scaling on multiple GPUs. The conclusions are bounded by the evaluated data scales, candidate budgets, and hardware platform; more complex real production workloads and multi-node distributed settings still require further validation.
\end{abstract*}
